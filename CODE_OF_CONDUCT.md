# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Peekie to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. We pledge to make participation harassment-free.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask when you are unsure.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they consider harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting tools on this repository or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. All reports are handled privately and taken seriously.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
